When tools meet craftsmanship

Charging 40,000 pesos for wedding coverage using just an iPhone 16 Pro Max and CapCut is ridiculous. Premium pricing demands premium tools, great storytelling and editing skills matter, yes, but a phone can’t replace the control, versatility, and quality of professional equipment. Skill and gear go hand in hand; asking that much for a phone setup undervalues both the craft and the client’s expectations.

The same applies in software development. Relying on AI or shortcuts might create something passable, but serious, reliable work requires solid foundations, proper tools, and mastery. Excellence isn’t free, and it isn’t made on shortcuts.
