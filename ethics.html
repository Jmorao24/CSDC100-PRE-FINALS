Responsible Use & Ethics

Ethical use of AI requires fairness, transparency, and responsibility. Users must understand how AI works and ensure that its outputs do not cause harm.

Responsible use in the academe

AI must support learning without replacing individual thinking. Students should properly acknowledge AI assistance and maintain academic honesty.

Avoiding plagiarism and misuse

AI content must be paraphrased, checked, and integrated with personal reasoning. Submitting fully AI-generated work without acknowledgment is academic dishonesty.

Accuracy and verification

Users must verify all AI outputs because the system may generate inaccurate or fabricated information.

Bias and fairness

AI models may inherit biases from training data. Users must avoid reinforcing stereotypes and must evaluate outputs critically.

Personal Guidelines

Use AI as a study aid, not as a substitute. Always verify facts, avoid dependency, maintain transparency, and prioritize integrity.
