FAQ — Generative AI

Generative artificial intelligence produces new outputs such as text, images, audio, and code by learning patterns from large datasets.

It uses deep learning systems that identify relationships in data and generate predictions based on probability models.

AI chat assistants, image generators, video editors, music generators, and coding assistants.

It can automate tasks, summarize information, create ideas quickly, support creativity, and enhance productivity.

It may generate incorrect, biased, or misleading outputs because it relies on learned patterns rather than true understanding.

It can support decisions but cannot replace human judgment in situations requiring ethics or responsibility.

Because they generate outputs based on probability, not comprehension, which leads to confident but incorrect answers.
